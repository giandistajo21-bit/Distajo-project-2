Charting Your Path to the Future

Academic and technical-vocational tracks

KCC Senior High School offers Academic and TVL tracks so students can follow the path that fits their interests and goals. Every track includes Catholic values formation and activities that develop the whole person.

Basic Education (Elementary & JHS)College Programs

Academic Track

For students preparing for college or university programs.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

For students who intend to pursue degrees in the sciences, engineering, technology, mathematics and related fields. It focuses on advanced mathematics, sciences and research.

Key Subjects

  • Pre-Calculus
  • Basic Calculus
  • General Biology
  • General Chemistry
  • General Physics
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Engineering · Medicine · IT/Computer Science · Architecture · Research

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management

Designed for students planning to pursue business-related courses such as business administration, accountancy, marketing and entrepreneurship.

Key Subjects

  • Fundamentals of ABM
  • Business Mathematics
  • Business Finance
  • Organization & Management
  • Applied Economics
  • Principles of Marketing

College Paths

Accountancy · Business Administration · Entrepreneurship · Marketing · Economics

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

For students interested in journalism, communication arts, liberal arts, education and other social science programs in college.

Key Subjects

  • Creative Writing
  • Creative Nonfiction
  • Introduction to World Religions
  • Trends, Networks, & Critical Thinking
  • Philippine Politics & Governance
  • Community Engagement

College Paths

Education · Law · Psychology · Mass Communication · Public Administration

GAS

General Academic Strand

A flexible strand for students who are still undecided on their college course. It covers a broad range of subjects from different academic tracks.

Key Subjects

  • Humanities
  • Social Sciences
  • Applied Economics
  • Organization & Management
  • Electives from other strands
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Any college program · Liberal Arts · Education · Business · Social Sciences
